# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics | Task 2

---

## Objective
Analyzed customer subscription data to identify churn patterns,
key retention drivers, and customer lifetime trends for a
subscription-based business.

---

## Dataset Details
- 400 customer records (Jan 2022 – Dec 2024)
- 35% churn rate in dataset
- 4 Subscription Plans: Basic, Standard, Premium, Enterprise
- 5 Regions | 5 Acquisition Channels
- 8 Churn Reasons tracked

---

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop — Dashboard & Visualizations
- Microsoft Excel — Data & Cohort Table

---

## KPIs Analyzed
| KPI | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| Churn Rate % | % of customers who cancelled |
| Retention Rate % | % of customers still active |
| Avg Customer Tenure | Average months before churn |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | Total MRR from active customers |
| Avg Support Tickets | Tickets raised per customer |

---

## Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards — Churn Rate, Retention Rate, Total MRR, Avg Tenure
- Cohort Retention Table — Retention % by quarter
- Bar Chart — Churn reasons breakdown
- Donut Chart — Churn by subscription plan
- Line Chart — Monthly churn trend over time
- Bar Chart — Churn by acquisition channel
- Scatter Plot — Tenure vs Support Tickets
- Slicers — Filter by Region, Plan, Channel, Year

---

## Key Insights
- Overall churn rate is 35%
- "Price too high" is the top churn reason
- Basic plan customers churn fastest (low tenure)
- High support ticket count strongly correlates with churn
- Customers inactive for 30+ days are at high churn risk
- Referral channel customers have the best retention

---

## Actionable Recommendations
1. Introduce loyalty discounts for customers at 6-month mark
2. Proactively contact customers with 3+ support tickets
3. Re-engage customers inactive for more than 14 days
4. Improve Basic plan value — highest churn segment
5. Invest more in Referral channel — best retention source
6. Add exit survey to capture churn reasons in real time
